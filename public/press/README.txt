Fossify press kit
=================

Included files
--------------
- fossify-mark.svg: square vector logo
- fossify-mark.png: square 2048 x 2048 PNG logo
- fossify-mark.webp: square 2048 x 2048 lossless WebP logo
- fossify-horizontal.svg: horizontal vector logo with outlined text
- fossify-horizontal.png: horizontal 2400 x 1000 PNG logo
- fossify-horizontal.webp: horizontal 2400 x 1000 lossless WebP logo

SVG files can be scaled without resolution loss.

Usage notes
-----------
- Keep the original proportions, colors, and spacing.
- Leave clear space around the logo.
- Use a background with enough contrast for the complete mark.
- Do not imply endorsement or an official partnership.
- Identify modified screenshots or app artwork when relevant.
